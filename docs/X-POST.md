## Long form

```
Contested claims get argued in replies. Nobody pays for being wrong, and nothing settles.

We built Hunch at #NEARHackathon. Each claim is a card in a swipe deck with its sources, the live YES share and the NEAR staked. Swipe right to stake YES, left for NO. The wallet signs a real stake on NEAR testnet. Anyone can attach a source with a stance, and a curator reviews them.

To settle, a NEAR AI Cloud model reads the claim and its evidence, never the odds. The contract resolves the market only after Sworn's on-chain registry verifies the model key and its signature over that exact request and response. An insufficient answer voids the market and every staker gets their own stake back. Winners claim a pro rata share of both pools.

Open the Adam Back card to see the ruling, the reasoning, the resolution transaction and the payout.

Try it: https://hunch-near.vercel.app

@NEARProtocol @NearBuilders
```
